Verification Workflow

How the Service Works

A structured three-step process connects university department administrators with public verification.

01

Certificate Issuance

An authorized administrator enters the student's full name, roll number, course of study, unique Certificate ID, and issue date through the secured dashboard.

02

Authoritative Record Storage

The record is registered in the database with an exact issuance timestamp, creating a persistent reference point that protects against unauthorized changes.

03

Instant Public Verification

Anyone in possession of the Certificate ID can perform a lookup to view the verified academic credential details and confirm authenticity in seconds.

Academic Integrity

Why Digital Verification Matters

Traditional paper certificates present practical challenges for universities, graduating students, and evaluating employers.

Tamper Resistance

Paper diplomas and basic PDF scans can be modified without obvious physical signs. Having a verified digital record ensures that academic achievements remain exactly as granted by the institution.

Immediate Turnaround

Employers verifying academic backgrounds typically wait days or weeks for manual registrar office responses. Online validation delivers immediate confirmation of credentials.

Reliable Credential Sharing

Graduates can share their unique certificate identifier with hiring teams and postgraduate institutions, knowing their qualifications can be confirmed at any time.

Institutional Trust

Academic institutions protect the value of the degrees they award by ensuring that only genuine recipients can claim completion of their academic programs.
